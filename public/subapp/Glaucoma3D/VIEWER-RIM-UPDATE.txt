Interactive glaucoma viewer — rim and connector update, 2026-10-20

Open glaucoma-eye-interactive.html in Chrome or Edge. Refresh an existing tab
to load this update. The viewer remains self-contained and works offline.

Changes:
- Watch the Change panel moved right of center; its connectors have a dark
  backing so they remain visible over the model.
- Disc selection follows a contour aligned with the native retinal image.
- Rim selection emphasizes only a narrow peripheral band, leaving the cup clear.
- Iris surface highlight is magenta; the rest of the interface retains cyan.
- Mobile label selections automatically open their description; Open/Close
  replaces the former Info button.
- Mobile optic nerve selection connects both the posterior bundle and the
  retinal cup. Each connector has one bend at roughly one third of its length.
- Lower local lighting, softer highlights and no nerve emission improve
  visibility of the posterior nerve and its white outer sheaths.

Validation:
- Interaction, material configuration and projected geometry checks passed at
  six viewport configurations, including dual mobile connectors and Open/Close.
- CPU image checks confirmed that the rim selection excludes central cup pixels
  and all reference cup contours fit inside the adjusted disc border.
- Actual browser/WebGL rendering was not verified by the editing agent because
  its browser tool does not permit file:// pages.
