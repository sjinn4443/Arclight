Quick guide

Method

Quick confrontation screen: sit opposite at eye level in good light. Test one eye at a time. Patient covers the non-tested eye; examiner closes the opposite eye.

✓ Seen central good
? Suspect faded or unsure
× Absent not seen

Test

In each quadrant ask the patient to keep looking at your nose, hold your hand still, flash 1 or 2 fingers and ask them to count.

Central: use finger counting or a red target if central loss is suspected.

Interpret

This is a 5-point bedside screen, not formal field testing. Re-check any ? before deciding.

Use the result with onset, history and RAPD; sudden onset, stroke/HA or flash/curtain symptoms need urgent referral.
