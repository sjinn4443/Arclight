Quick guide

Glaucoma risk

Use this when examination information is limited: you select the closest disc appearance, IOP or palpation and the risk signs you observed. The app gives an approximate risk and referral prompt; it does not diagnose glaucoma.

Basics

C/Dcup size? IOPpressure? Riskother signs?

More detail

C/D: match the closest optic cup/disc ratio.

IOP: use measured pressure when available, or palpation if not.

Risk: rim signs, fields, pupils, VA and risk factors refine the result.

Teaching aid, not final diagnosis. Details combined are powerful.

How scoring works

Grid: IOP + C/D; rock palp = emergency warning.

IOP: ≤20 +0 | 21-24 +1 | 25-29 +2 | ≥30 +3.

Add: rim +1; fields +1; pupils +0.5; VA +0.25-1; risks +0.2 each.

Shift: add-ons ≥2 move one row up; disc S +2/right, L -2/left.

    Painful red eye + sudden visual loss: emergency assessment. Do not wait for the grid.

    Gauge disc size:

    • Smaller - blurred, raised, 'crowded'
    • Larger - clear margins, crescented, pigmented

    Larger discs may have larger cups. Near-total cupping still needs escalation.

    • Thin rim/notch - advanced tissue loss
    • Haemorrhage - nerve fibre damage
    • Visible lamina cribrosa - deep cupping
    • Odd or displaced vessels - structural changes or abnormal branching
    • Pigment changes at disc margin - myopia or chorioretinal atrophy
    • Baring of circumlinear vessels - thinning of neuroretinal rim
    • Bayoneting - advanced cupping
