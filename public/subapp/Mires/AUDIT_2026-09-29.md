# Mires audit — 29 September 2026

Initial review followed by user-authorised implementation. The seven confirmed findings below are corrected. Newton scoring is preserved with clearer tolerance feedback. Regression evidence: 11 unit/contracts tests, exact bundle parity, established browser smoke and new HTTP/direct-file touch checks pass. Direct-file fonts still use Chromium's fallback. Clinical and physical-device acceptance remain pending. The findings below describe the pre-fix baseline.

## Confirmed findings

1. **High: rendered centre differs from the assessed centre.** At 360 × 740 the mire wrapper is 180 × 144 while its SVG is 180 × 180. The SVG centre is 18px below the crosshair at zero displacement. A completed Variable IOP case nevertheless reports `Centre OK`. Align the SVG and wrapper coordinate systems before adjusting tolerances.
2. **High: maximum zoom breaks the movement bounds.** At zoom 2 the mire box exceeds the blue circle. The lower and upper bounds in `constrainMiresToCircle()` become inverted, forcing displacement to -80px horizontally and -44px vertically in the recorded case. Define bounds that remain valid when the graphic exceeds the aperture and reassess alignment when zoom changes.
3. **High: thickness changes can leave the visual separation stale.** The thickness input updates stroke width without calling `updateSeparation()`, although the separation calculation depends on stroke width. Newton mode and touch Variable mode lack the desktop periodic separation refresh. Recorded stroke width changed from 10 to 20 while the semicircle translation stayed at 38.85. Refresh geometry and assessment together.
4. **Clinical teaching contradiction:** the information popup says excess NaFl gives thick mires and high bias while Primary MCQs teach thick mires with possible under-reading and thin mires with possible over-reading. These cannot remain opposing unqualified rules. Review fluorescein concentration, visible mire width and tear-film volume separately against the cited sources before changing answer keys. This audit does not provide clinical sign-off.
5. **Medium: Drift is ineffective on touch devices.** Separation drift is conditional on `!isMobileDevice()`. The visible control remains available on touch devices, including potentially touch-enabled laptops. In a touch-enabled browser, setting Drift to 5 left separation unchanged during the recorded interval.
6. **Medium: training panel keyboard behaviour is incomplete.** Escape leaves the Newton panel open. Opening and closing these panels lack the expected focus transfer and restoration.
7. **Medium: incomplete MCQ submission focuses the result message rather than the first unanswered question.** Submission is correctly blocked but focus does not follow the fleet standard.

## Decisions rather than confirmed faults

- Newton accepts answers within 2 mmHg of a band's nearest edge. This is established behaviour, not an arithmetic error. `Within tolerance` would be clearer than `Correct` when the selected band excludes the actual value.
- Some Advanced MCQs revisit basic technique. Review difficulty progression separately without changing established pass marks.
- The Newton 20/25/30 controls are teaching weights, not scored answer choices. Their separate purpose should be preserved.
