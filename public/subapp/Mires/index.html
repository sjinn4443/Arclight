Goldmann Quick Guide

Keep the endpoint simple: Centre / Touch / Steady.

Goldmann estimates IOP from force needed to flatten a 3.06 mm corneal area.

Use topical anaesthetic plus a small amount of fluorescein.

Too much NaFl: thick mires, high bias. Too little: thin mires, low bias.

Endpoint: centre the mires, make the inner edges just touch and hold steady.

Avoid lid/finger pressure on the globe; keep patient relaxed and looking straight ahead.
