## Confirmed defects

1. **Blank VA can produce routine screening.** In `src/triage.js`, `getVaRisk('')` returns none and `evaluateEye` can return routineScreen without distanceVA. Two clear disc/macula views with no-signs selected return Routine (screening) with both VAs blank. Require the necessary recorded data before reassuring completion; preserve red-flag precedence.
2. **Same-eye poor-view limitations are dropped.** Microaneurysms plus an ungradable right view returns Routine (weeks) but the overall limitations omit the right poor view. The aggregation excludes limitations at the winning priority. This also affects macula-risk and urgent eyes. Keep the higher-priority action while retaining all material view limitations and repeat-view needs.
3. **Teaching dilation changes the patient record.** `viewerDilationToggle` calls `setClinicalDilation`, which writes state.dilation and referral content. Initial dilation is also `no`, so an untouched report states not dilated rather than not recorded. This conflicts with the popup's simulation-only claim. Viewing mode also mutates recorded area validity; define separate teaching and examination ownership before correcting.
4. **Untouched referral findings become `none`.** `src/referral-note.js` formats an empty findings object as none instead of not recorded. Explicit no-signs selection must remain distinct from unanswered findings.
5. **Mobile control presentation is cramped.** At 360 × 740 the cataract control is approximately 92 × 79px. None/Slight/Med/Dense stack vertically in a narrow column instead of reading as slider stops. The Skin control and neighbouring switches have uneven heights. No horizontal document overflow was measured, but control hierarchy needs a narrow layout correction.

## Clinical teaching and coverage needing a decision

- All NPDR signs, including venous beading, share one routine action. The UI cannot record extent or IRMA and cannot distinguish severe NPDR. This is a limitation of the simplified model, not proof that its weeks timing is too slow. Do not invent new local referral intervals. ICO guidance treats severity explicitly, including in low-resource settings: https://www.aaojournal.org/article/S0161-6420%2817%2933523-6/fulltext and https://icoph.org/eye-care-delivery/diabetic-eye-care/.
- Reduced VA without DR defaults to routine referral and there is no onset/symptom input. Clearly limit the tool to its screening role and avoid implying that this assesses sudden visual loss.
- Holo widens/reduces the image and removes the corneal reflex but the fundus draw path has no BIO inversion. Confirm the intended Holo device orientation before changing artwork or applying a generic BIO model. Do not blindly transplant Morph's implementation.
- MCQ banks have 16/26/26 questions and attempts of 5/6/8. Option order is shuffled, incomplete submission is blocked and focus goes to the first unanswered item. Nevertheless many Intermediate/Advanced items repeat basic priority rules, and several distractors are implausible. One question asks for the reason for non-dilation, but no such recording control is present. Clinical classification/source review does not independently validate the app's local timings.
