Quick guide

Diabetic retinopathy

Practise with the 10 retinal image cases, then record RE and LE separately. This is a teaching aid and triage prompt, not a screening result.

Arclight (DO) is a small direct view. Holo (BIO) is a wider view. Gaze simulates a patient moving their eye. Dilation increases the field of view. Cataract adds slight, med or dense blur. Darker skin also gives darker fundus pigmentation.

More detail

DR signs: MA, dot/blot haemorrhage, CWS or venous beading.

Macula risk: hard exudates near the macula, foveal risk or reduced VA with DR signs.

Red flags: NVD, NVE, preretinal haemorrhage or vitreous haemorrhage need same-day referral.

View only: gaze, cataract and skin controls alter the simulation, not the diagnosis.

No signs means no referable signs were seen in the view obtained.

Routine diabetic eye screening is still required.
