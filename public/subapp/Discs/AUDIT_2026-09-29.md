## Confirmed findings

1. **BIO orientation missing.** `src/viewer.js:1359` mirrors the fellow eye but does not apply the retinal 180-degree rotation used by Diabetic's Holo/BIO branch. Edge labels at line 1723 also ignore mode. Correct retinal rotation and nasal/temporal labelling together, preserving anterior/corneal overlays. A horizontal flip alone is not the correct BIO transformation.
2. **Blank VA can yield routine reassurance.** With both eyes clear, disc+macula recorded and no signs selected, both VA fields can remain blank while `evaluateTriage` returns Routine disc check. `src/triage.js:209–219` does not require recorded VA for the reassuring branches. Preserve urgent results with incomplete information but qualify incomplete low-risk assessments.
3. **Winning-eye limited view disappears.** Swollen right disc plus hazy right view gives urgent action with no limitations. Lines 298 and 338 exclude urgent eyes and equal/highest-priority eyes. Retain poor-view limitations independently of priority.
4. **Teaching and examination context are coupled.** `script.js:649` records the viewer dilation toggle as patient dilation. Mode changes at line 875 also alter examination mode and can clear recorded area through `src/state.js:49`. Reset keeps that coupled state. Diabetic has already separated these concerns.
5. **Untouched report implies negative findings.** `src/state.js:15` defaults dilation to no and `src/referral-note.js` emits findings: none for unrecorded eyes. Use unrecorded wording rather than inferred negatives.
6. **Mobile cataract loses optical effects.** `src/viewer.js:622–632` returns brightness only on mobile, dropping blur, contrast and saturation. Diabetic retains the same shortcut; Swollen Discs has been corrected. Compare perceptual and performance behaviour before sharing the corrected implementation.
7. **Image failure is silent.** `src/viewer.js:280` tries a JPEG fallback then returns without an explicit visible error. A loaded case title must not imply a usable requested image. Add loading/error handling and verify delayed/failing replacements as in Swollen Discs. This item is source-confirmed; failure injection was not run in this pass.
8. **Compact controls need a measured layout correction.** The 360 x 740 screenshot shows the cataract labels stacked into a cramped column and the skin control stretched vertically. Apply the existing Diabetic control arrangement rather than shrinking the text. Main page and closed guide currently fit without scrolling.

- Drusen uncertainty is correctly described in help and MCQs as potentially swelling, but the selected drusen branch is routine. Keep that exception discoverable at the result.

## Cross-app orientation contract

DO retinal imagery stays upright. BIO retinal imagery is inverted and laterally reversed, equivalent to a 180-degree rotation. RE/LE mirroring is a separate transformation. Labels must follow the retinal display, not merely eye side. Swollen Discs has no BIO selector and now avoids artificial vertical flipping in its direct-view timed rounds. Fundal Reflex has no retinal BIO image to rotate.

Primary optical reference: https://www.aao.org/young-ophthalmologists/yo-info/article/indirect-ophthalmoscopy-101 and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16382844/.
