Quick guide

Optic disc assessment

Use the cases for practice, or while examining a patient record the disc view and signs you actually see for RE and LE. The app suggests a triage action; it does not diagnose the cause.

Arclight (DO) gives a small direct view; Holo (BIO) gives a wider view. Gaze, dilation, cataract and skin controls change only the simulated view.

More detail

Case sets: general disc signs, normal cups and glaucoma sequence.

General disc signs: swelling, pallor, drusen, anomalous discs, myelination and peripapillary change.

Cup context: C/D 0.3 and disc size help interpretation but do not trigger referral by themselves.

Glaucoma disc signs: thin rim, rim notch, splinter haemorrhage, exposed lamina, C/D 0.9 and vessel changes need fast glaucoma review.

Red flags: true swelling, acute visual loss or abnormal pupils need urgent review.

View only: gaze, cataract and skin controls alter the simulation, not the diagnosis.

No signs means no referable disc signs were seen in the view obtained.

Symptoms, pupils, fields and local pathways still matter.
