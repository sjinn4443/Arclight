# Morph clinical review status

Status date: 23 July 2026

Morph is a teaching-only simulator. This engineering pass preserved its existing teaching text, condition image set, viewer mathematics and interaction behaviour. It did not validate the educational accuracy of the retinal depictions or establish diagnostic use.

- Independent clinical reviewer: not assigned
- Independent clinical content review: pending
- Clinical deployment approval: not granted or inferred
- Engineering regression verification: complete locally

Any future change to condition wording, image interpretation, optical behaviour or teaching claims requires an appropriately qualified reviewer and a recorded outcome.

## MCQ exclusion review — 26 July 2026

No MCQ was added. Morph does not calculate a clinical action or referral result and its five conditions are simulator inputs, so a quiz would extend the educational scope without a separately reviewed question bank. The Cup remains awarded for visiting Normal, Swollen disc, Cupped disc, CRVO and AMD. Engineering contracts protect that completion rule. The condition artwork and teaching wording still require independent clinical review.

# Teaching corrections — 29 September 2026

BIO fundus orientation now uses a 180-degree rotation, matching the inverted/reversed image described by AAO EyeWiki: https://eyewiki.aao.org/Binocular_Indirect_Ophthalmoscopy. A calibrated BIO optical model is not claimed: field sizes remain illustrative and Rx scaling is restricted to Direct. Mobile cataract filtering now retains the desktop blur/contrast/saturation effects. No artwork or diagnosis labels changed. Independent clinical sign-off and physical-device acceptance remain pending.
