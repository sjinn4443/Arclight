Quick guide

What it shows

Use this simulator to practise direct ophthalmoscopy and BIO views: you move the view over the model eye, adjust the controls and choose a condition to see how it appears. It is not a patient examination or diagnosis.

Conditionretinal image Cataractnone to dense Fielddirect + BIO Rx+++ to ---

Using the view

Field: illustrative sizes. Direct is upright; BIO is inverted and reversed.

Cataract: adds blur, tint, contrast loss and patchy opacity.

Child: adds less steady movement and a livelier view.

Rx scaling is illustrative and Direct-only. Field size and magnification are not calibrated.
