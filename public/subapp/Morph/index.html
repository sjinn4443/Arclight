Quick guide

What it shows

Morph simulates how DO and BIO fundus views change with cataract, field size, refractive error and patient movement. It is a teaching aid, not a diagnostic tool.

Conditionretinal image Cataractnone to dense Fielddirect + BIO Rx+++ to ---

Using the view

Field: 5, 8 and 15 deg are DO. 25, 35 and 45 deg are BIO.

Cataract: adds blur, tint, contrast loss and patchy opacity.

Child: adds less steady movement and a livelier view.

Wider fields make orientation easier. Narrow fields make small changes harder to inspect.
