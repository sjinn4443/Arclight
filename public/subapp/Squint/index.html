About

Use this while observing the patient: you drag each iris to match the eye position you see. Use the Gaze tracker for conjugate movement, cover controls for fixation responses and pupil controls for light and near responses. It supports teaching; it does not make a final diagnosis.

More detail

Alignment: drag an iris. Gaze tracker: moves both eyes and shows muscle demand; RE is screen-left, LE screen-right.

Muscles: SR superior rectus · IR inferior rectus · MR medial rectus · LR lateral rectus · SO superior oblique · IO inferior oblique.

Light + pupils: use the swinging-light control. Hold Near for pupil constriction and convergence.

Cover: cover-uncover observes manifest re-fixation. Alternate cover reveals latent or total deviation.

Use Primary, Intermediate and Advanced presets for structured practice.

Hold about 3 seconds per eye, swing steadily and keep timing equal.
