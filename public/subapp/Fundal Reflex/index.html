Quick guide

Fundal reflex

The fundal reflex is the glow in the pupil from the fundus, seen with Arclight at arm's length. We use fundal reflex rather than red reflex. In those with darker pigmentation, a normal reflex may look orange-yellow or blue-white. Bright, equal and round is reassuring. Dim light, calm patient. Compare both eyes for brightness, colour and shape. Move side to side, then closer.

Basics

Matchsame both sides? Brightclear reflex? Straighteyes aligned?

More detail

Light + colour: brightness, opacity, orange-red or blue-white.

Shape + crescent: size, roundness, notches, upper or lower crescent.

Cornea + compare: centration, sharpness and the other eye.
