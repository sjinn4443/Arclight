Ocular Trauma Score

Purpose

Use this after an eye-injury assessment: you select the presenting VA and risk findings you observed. The app estimates visual prognosis using its configured OTS-style calculation. It does not determine treatment urgency or replace trauma management.

Published OTS estimates are about 80% accurate for mechanical injuries. This app excludes chemical, electrical, heat and major facial injuries.

Use

Select VA then pick risks -> computed score, category number and table are shown.

Reference

Read in-depth CEHJ article and always use calm and methodical judgement.
