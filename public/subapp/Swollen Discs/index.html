Quick guide

Use this simulator to compare a disc with normal, suspicious and swollen examples: you select the appearance that best matches what you see and practise referral recognition. It does not grade or diagnose a patient's disc.

Basics

Clear edge Cup easy to see
Raised edge Halo or swelling
Vessels hidden Check view quality

Start with Normal. Then compare Suspicious and Swollen. Scan around the disc before deciding.

Suspicious swelling needs same-day advice; definite swelling needs emergency review. Poor views or haemorrhage alone do not establish the grade.

Use the view slider to practise normal and wide views.

Cataract makes the view harder. It is not part of the disc grade.

Timed sets reuse three examples: practice, not clinical certification.
