Pragmatic Diabetic Retinopathy Screening

This is a non-traditional, pragmatic diabetic eye screening strategy designed for general primary and secondary level health care settings.

It aims to integrate screening into general medical and non-communicable disease (NCD) clinics.

Mid-level eye care and some general health care workers can deliver this screening using the Arclight Package.
