Childhood Vision Screening Questions

Any concerns with vision / appearance of eyes?

Observe and assess visual development

observe for...

  • Normal visual behaviours
  • Signs of visual impairment
  • Pupils black, round and equal
  • Squint or wobbly eyes
  • Red eye, discharge, or squeezing eyes shut
  • Follows your face, and from how far
  • Returns a silent smile
  • Follows a light
  • Reaches accurately
  • Objects to one eye being covered
