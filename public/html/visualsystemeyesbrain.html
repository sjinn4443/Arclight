Eye & Brain

The eyes collect light
from the world and turn
this into signals for the brain
to create ‘vision’.

Light is focused onto
the back of the eye
by the cornea and lens.

Cornea

Lens

At the back of the eye
is the retina.

Retina

This is like a sensor or film
in a camera which turns the
light into electrical signals
that are passed to the brain
down the optic nerve.

The brain then creates
vision from these signals.
