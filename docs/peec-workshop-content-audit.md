## Primary Ear Care Workshop

Route: `#/primaryEarCareWorkshop`.

It uses the PEC folder layout and primary theme, keyboard-accessible lesson rows, two levels of folder navigation, Previous/Next within the current folder, completion ticks and restoration of the folder on return. It is linked from PEC and the Ears catalogue. A **Direct to PEC Workshop** button appears beneath the workshop.

17 lesson rows:

- **1. Introduction**
  - Getting Started: Objectives; Aim; What is Arclight?
  - Ear Health and Hearing Impairment: Ear Health and Hearing Impairment; Ear anatomy
- **2. Ear Examination**
  - History: History
  - Test hearing: Testing hearing in children; Child visual development: Workshop video
  - Otoscopy: Observe/palpate/otoscopy; Otoscopy examination video; Otoscopy poster and guide; Common ear conditions; Practice on each other; Simulation Tools: Three Stations
- **3. Primary Ear Care Procedures**
  - Ear washout and irrigation
  - Dry mopping or wicking
  - Instillation of ear drops

The video embedded in slide 135 illustrates **visual development**, despite its location in the deck's ear section. The app labels it accordingly. The hearing-development and hearing-testing handouts are separate lessons in the same folder.

The source provides the names of the three ear procedures, but does not provide procedural videos or detailed step-by-step instructions. These rows are supervised practice outlines; no clinical procedural details or nonexistent videos were invented.
