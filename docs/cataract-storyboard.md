# Cataract examination storyboard

Read each numbered row of the one-page Cataract_Edit5 handout from left to right. The clinician demonstrates a test, the centre shows its illustrated response and the right shows a finding that changes assessment or referral. Read each symbol in its row. In particular, the four centre pictures in row 5 show lens opacity. Their green heading does not establish normality.
